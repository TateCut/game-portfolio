    How to Play

    Climb one word at a time.

    • Unscramble the letters into a word.
    • Each new word adds one letter.

    Find the theme words.

    • Each Climb hides a word that fits today's theme.
    • Theme words turn yellow and are worth 2 points.

    No cheap steps.

    • Adding just -s, -d, -r or -n doesn't count.
    • 3 wrong words end the Climb. 1 hint a day.
